# EtherVerse (EV)

Ether's project universe: every app orbits Ether's sun.

**Visit:** https://tonyparker1964.github.io/EtherVerse/

## The worlds
- **Ether's Field** (Venus): a sound-reactive visualizer with cymatics, chakra tones and a live solar system. [Open](https://tonyparker1964.github.io/Ethers-field/)
- **Ether Beat Lab** (Jupiter): a browser beat maker with nine genres, pads, a sampler and exports for Ether Cut. [Open](https://tonyparker1964.github.io/Ether-Beat-Lab/)
- **Ether Cut** (Neptune): a browser music-video editor. [Code](https://github.com/tonyparker1964/Ether-s-Cut)
- **Ether Image Studio** (Uranus): 146 looks from around the world, blended into image and motion prompts. [Open](https://tonyparker1964.github.io/Ether-Image-Studio/)
- **Ether Explains** (Mercury): build 3D animated explainers with Claude and three.js, then record them with your voiceover, in the browser or the Mac app. [Open](https://tonyparker1964.github.io/Ether-Explains/)
- **Ether's Celestial Atlas** (Eris): all 88 constellations and 5,044 stars on a sphere you can turn. [Open](https://tonyparker1964.github.io/Ether-Celestial-Atlas/)
- **Ether Voice Reader** (Makemake): a reader for whole books and passages that narrates in your own voice on your Mac, plus a browser reader and Voice Studio. [Code](https://github.com/tonyparker1964/Ether-Voice-Reader)
- **Ethers Prism** (Haumea): a Mac app that turns a goal into an editor of dials, for text, images, Blender 3D, logos and beats, with local models or your own Claude and ChatGPT accounts. [Code](https://github.com/tonyparker1964/Ethers-Prism)
- **Lemurian Reimagined** (Quaoar): seven zones of a living civilization, with myth, history and engineering kept apart. [Open](https://tonyparker1964.github.io/Lemurian-Reimagined/)
- **Ether's Magnetric Universe** (Sedna): a journey inward through eighteen worlds, one for each chapter of Magnetric Being. [Open](https://tonyparker1964.github.io/Ether-s-Magnetric-Universe-/)
- **Ether's Cymatic** (Ceres): words, tones, songs and your voice turned into glowing cymatic patterns in sand, water and light. [Open](https://tonyparker1964.github.io/EtherCymatic/)
- **LightBody** (Gonggong): a symbolic 114-point energy body that lights up point by point. [Code](https://github.com/tonyparker1964/LightBody)
- **Ether's View** (Pluto): a journey through the universe from the Big Bang to the farthest known worlds. [Open](https://tonyparker1964.github.io/Ether-View/)
- **The Zero's Journey** (Earth, with the Moon as v2): a personal-growth app in nine stages. Private.
- **Tribe Xchange** (Mars): a SwiftUI app for community trading with time tokens. Private.
- **Alignment** (Saturn, with Titan as 1.0): a personal app. Private.

The public projects in "All repositories" update live from the GitHub API.
