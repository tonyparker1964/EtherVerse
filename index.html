Ether’s Field

Feel your music as living light.

A sound-reactive visualizer for musicians, meditators and visual artists. Sand settles into cymatic figures, flames dance to the kick drum, and a live solar system lines the planets up on any date from 1800 to 2050.

  • Stellar Alignment: real NASA orbits, planet meanings and sky reports
  • Cymatic plates in sand, fire, water, air and ether
  • Chakra colours and tones from 396 to 963 Hz
  • Beat detection and a guided breath pace
  • Records video with sound, straight from the screen
  • Installs on a phone and works offline

Ether Beat Lab

Press randomize, find a groove, shape it by hand.

A beat maker where every sound is synthesized in the browser. Nine genres, 53 instruments and a twelve-track sequencer, with WAV and timing-marker exports that drop straight into Ether Cut.

  • Boom Bap, Lo-fi, Trap, Drill, House, Afrobeats and more
  • One-press randomize, with locks for what you keep
  • Ether Voices, Atmos and World instruments
  • Sixteen pads and an MPC-style sampler
  • World scales such as Hijaz, Bhairav and Pelog
  • Exports WAV and beat markers for Ether Cut

Ether Cut

Cut a music video to the beat, in the browser.

Drop in videos, images and a song, arrange them on a timeline or storyboard, time the scenes to the music and export a finished video. Nothing is uploaded.

  • Timeline and storyboard, in 21:9, 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1
  • Tap to mark scenes on the beat
  • Keyframes, layers and blend modes
  • GPU looks: glow, light leak, grain, glitch, kaleidoscope
  • Particles, beat-reactive pulses and animated text
  • Exports MP4 or WebM

Ether Image Studio

146 looks from around the world. Blend up to four.

Choose image styles, 3D looks, Japanese anime, donghua, world styles and Ether Visions, then set the scene, characters, location, light and film look. The studio writes a still-image prompt and a matching motion prompt to animate it.

  • Blend 2, 3 or 4 looks, or let it surprise you
  • 570 locations in 12 categories
  • A saved cast of characters with reference sheets
  • 33 anime looks by era, genre and approach
  • Image and motion prompts, ready to copy
  • Everything saved in your browser, with backup

About Ether

Ether is a state of mind, and the expression of whatever medium carries the art.

Ether makes AI art and music in many voices, writes books drawn from lived experience, and animates the characters of Zero’s Journey, where Ether travels with Element, a tiger who changes form with each world.

The apps here grew out of that work. They are the tools Ether wanted for making music videos, finding visual styles, and sitting quietly with sound and light.
